Privacy policy

Short and plain: what data this website processes about you, why, and what I do with it.

Effective from 29 October 2026

Who is responsible

The controller is Aleš Pospíšil (CORPHIK), email ales@corphik.eu.

The “Write to me” form

What data
Your name (optional), email or phone, your message and the services in your cart.
Why
So I can reply to your request and agree on a price and date with you.
Legal basis
Steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
How long
While your request and any work together are being handled, and at most 3 years after our last contact. Then I delete the message.
Who receives it
The message is sent through Web3Forms, which delivers it to my mailbox. I don't sell your data or pass it on to other companies.

The same applies if you email me directly.

Visiting the website

Hosting. The site runs on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). As with any website, your IP address and technical details about your browser are processed to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. See GitHub's privacy statement.

No third-party services. Fonts, images and scripts are hosted on this website. Nothing is loaded from Google or other third-party servers.

No cookies. Your cart and chosen language are stored only in your browser (localStorage). They are never sent anywhere, and clearing the site data in your browser removes them.

Your rights

You can ask me for access to your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict processing or to transfer it, and you can object to processing. Just write to ales@corphik.eu.

If you think I'm not handling your data properly, you can complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ), or to the authority in your country.
